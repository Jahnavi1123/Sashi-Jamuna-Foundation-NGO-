Our Heritage & Mission

About Sashi Jamuna Foundation

Founded in homage to enduring familial values of service, benevolence, and community preservation in Bihar.

Our Origin & Guiding Light

Sashi Jamuna Foundation was established with a singular, resolute objective: to uplift vulnerable communities in rural Bihar while preserving the venerable cultural heritage of Mithila.

Named in memory of visionary community elders whose life philosophy revolved around sharing whatever little one possessed with those in greater need, the foundation has evolved into a structured NGO empowering thousands of lives across education, healthcare, nutrition, and indigenous artisan upliftment.

"True empowerment does not merely offer aid; it restores dignity, builds self-reliant livelihoods, and honors our ancestral roots."

Our Vision

A thriving Bihar where every child has access to quality education, every village possesses dependable medical care, and every traditional Mithila artisan earns an honorable, sustainable livelihood through their ancestral craft.

Our Mission

To implement ground-level grassroots initiatives with 100% financial transparency, partnering directly with local panchayats, women self-help collectives, and medical volunteers to create systemic, multi-generational change.

Ground Action

Our Strategic Initiatives

Focused, measurable programs delivering life-changing impact across Bihar.

Project 01 • Education

Shiksha Jyoti: Girl-Child & Youth Literacy

Our flagship educational program operates after-school learning centers across Madhubani and Darbhanga districts. We provide bridge courses, digital literacy tablets, textbooks, nutritious midday snacks, and full secondary school fee coverage for first-generation girl students.

4,800+ Students Enrolled
28 Smart Centers
98% Pass Rate
Project 02 • Heritage & Women

Mithila Kala Shakti: Women Artisan Guild

Madhubani painting is a globally celebrated UNESCO recognized art form, yet grassroots women artists often struggle with fair remuneration. We provide high-grade handmade paper, natural dye preparation workshops, design incubation, and direct exhibition access.

1,280+ Certified Painters
3.2x Income Surge
100% Women Led
Project 03 • Health & Hygiene

Gramin Arogya Sarthi: Mobile Healthcare

Bringing primary diagnosis, basic surgical triage, clean delivery kits, and sanitary pad distribution to remote floodplain villages with limited transport links.

165+ Health Camps
8,900+ Eye Screenings
15,000+ Hygiene Kits

Field Dispatch Today, 11:30 AM • Madhubani Center

Orientation Completed for Batch #14 of Mithila Folk Apprentices

Today, we welcomed 40 young women from Rajnagar block into our 6-month certified Bharni and Kachni style Madhubani painting apprenticeship. All trainees received initial raw material kits, bamboo nib pens, and natural color sets funded by our monthly donor circle.

Posted by: Renu Jha (Field Coordinator)
Healthcare Mission Yesterday • Darbhanga Rural

Eye Screening & Free Spectacle Distribution Camp

In collaboration with Patna Medical Alumni volunteers, our mobile medical van conducted vision tests for 320 elderly citizens. 184 prescription spectacles were custom-fitted and distributed completely free of charge. 22 patients requiring cataract surgery have been scheduled for next week.

Posted by: Dr. Alok Kumar (Medical Lead)
