Who We Are

A foundation built on compassion, culture and community.

संस्कृति और सेवा — culture and service — sit at the heart of everything we do. Founded in Bihar, the Sashi Jamuna Foundation brings together volunteers, educators, health workers, artisans and donors to build dignity and opportunity in the places that need it most.

[Editable placeholder — add the foundation's founding story, year of registration and founding trustees here from the admin dashboard.]

  • Education first — learning support, libraries and digital classrooms for children in rural schools.

  • Health & dignity — camps, awareness drives and maternal health support for families.

  • Living heritage — training women artisans in traditional Madhubani painting for sustainable income.
